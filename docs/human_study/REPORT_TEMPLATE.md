## Interpretation and limitations

- Does the primary contrast support the predeclared practically meaningful effect? What does its uncertainty permit or rule out?
- Did accuracy, time and the two reliance measures move in compatible or conflicting directions?
- Could carryover, case difficulty, model-error oversampling, interface layout or missingness explain the pattern?
- What population, case distribution and task does the study actually support?
- What would require a new study rather than an extrapolation?

Separate verified findings from speculation. State null, adverse and inconclusive outcomes as clearly as positive ones.
